## The Roadmap ,

Here are the few things you need to set up before starting your investment journey. Please go by this particular order only. This is more import than investment itself, take this SERIOUSLY.

---

- 1. Emergency fund
- 2. Health Insurence
- 3. Life Insurence
- 4. Investment

---

## 1. Emergency fund

This is the fund you go to if you have any emergency. Make sure this fund is very safe. Don’t chase for returns in this fund. Don’t take any risk here. Any return that is just even slightly above the inflation rate is perfectly fine. Have around 6 months of expenses here. Make sure liquidity is there where you park this fund. Prefer funds like Arbitrage funds , debt instruments , FDs etc., since they have low risk. You must refill this fund every time you cash out from here as early as possible. Also one should take amount from this fund only at an emergency not frequent low needs.

## 2. Life Insurance

Opt out from any insurance that combines investment with insurance eg like LIC. Go for pure life insurance (Term Insurance) because they offer large sum assured for a relatively lower premium. Opt for Term Insurance as early as possible since for every birthday the premium increases. They also offer tax benefit. The beneficiary can be changed at any time. While choosing term plan go for yearly pay, until life time, don’t get excited by return of premium, don’t opt for unnecessary riders, go via third party like policy bazaar since claim assistance will be easy. 60-71 yrs is just fine. how to choose right sum assured ? ## 3. Health Insurance

If you are from IT , fully educate yourself of the health insurance policy that you have in the company. opt for your parents health insurance only if they already don’t have one. but you have to also have a health insurance outside company in case you lost your job. If you don’t have any such plan or started your career just now, I personally think there is no urgency for it now, the group insurance is fine. But you have to have a Health Insurance outside of the company as well.

## 4. Investment

Once you have all those 3 above set up lets start with 4. Investment. One must understand the difference between trading and investment first: Trading is short term, the primary purpose is profit alone. Investment is focused on a longer window, not only for wealth generation but also for wealth protection. There are many options for an individual to invest. I am broadly classifying them into these topics. There are some others like crypto, forex etc., But let’s not go there. I am not considering peer to peer options like chit funds since there are no government regulations involved and trust is also a major risk factor.

- Equity
- Debt Instruments
- Gold
- Real Estate
- Government Schemes
