In Technical analysis , price is the Essence.

## Fundamentals of Technical Analysis

First we need to understand demand and supply in the market. These demand and supply alter the prices. The demand and supply are in the form of buyers and sellers. Here is the rule of thumb:

More buyers  = Rise in price

More sellers   = Fall in price

Lets understand this rule of thumb. More buys means increase in quantity demanded, increase in quantity demanded leads to price control by sellers, the seller rise price for profit and thus price increases as buyers increase. More sellers means increase in quantity supplied, increase in quantity supplied leads to price control by buyers, the buyers offer to buy at low prices and thus price decreases as sellers increase. For example if there is an panic buy then price of the stock increases in short term resulting in overvaluation.
