## How to account a share ?

- Face Value – Fixed nominal value assigned by the company for accounting. Remains constant until share split occurs.
- Book Value – NetWorth of the company per share
- Market Value – Price at which the share is currently trading

## IPO

Initial Public Offering – Private company sells shares to public for the first time to raise fund, turning from private business to publicly traded company in a stock exchange.

Process of an IPO

– Step 1 ; Company decides to go public for fund

– Step 2 ; Company files a DRHP (Draft Red Herring Prospectus) with SEBI. DRHP has details of the IPO and the companies past financial performance disclosed.

–  Step 3 ; Company chooses a Stock Exchange to list its shares. NSE/BSE/Both

– Step 4 ; IPO Promotion

– Step 5 ; Bidding window opens for the investors to bid

– Step 6 ; Shares allotment to investors after the window closes

– Step 7 ; Share price is set with the help of underwriters. Underwriters are middleman between company and public.

– Step 8 ; Share is listed and can be traded by public.
